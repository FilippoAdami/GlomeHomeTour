# 06_mesh_refinment — Stage 8: Mesh Refinement & Decimation (Staging)

`06_mesh_refinment` is the final mesh post-processing and geometric optimization stage in the GlomeHomeTour reconstruction pipeline. It refines raw triangle meshes generated by `05_2DGS_to_mesh` into clean, manifold, optimized digital assets ready for real-time mobile/web rendering and MLS architectural compliance.

---

## What This Stage Does

1. **Topological Manifold Verification:** Resolves non-manifold edges, isolated vertices, duplicate faces, and self-intersections to guarantee 3D watertightness.
2. **Quadric Error Metric (QEM) Decimation:** Simplifies high-density meshes into target face budgets (e.g. 50k, 150k, 500k polygons) while strictly preserving sharp architectural corners and boundary silhouette edges.
3. **Level of Detail (LOD) Generation:** Computes multi-resolution LOD pyramids (LOD0 to LOD3) for progressive streaming across varying network conditions.
4. **Normal Smoothing & Edge Preservation:** Applies bilateral normal filtering across planar walls while preserving sharp $90^\circ$ room corners.
5. **Texture Atlas Repacking:** Merges and repacks disconnected texture islands into unified square power-of-two atlases ($2048 \times 2048$ or $4096 \times 4096$) with mipmap generation and basis universal compression.
